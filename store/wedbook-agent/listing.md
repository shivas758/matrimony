## Full description (max 4000)
Wedbook Agent is the workspace for marriage bureau agents. Register the families you work with, find matching profiles from your communities, and share them safely — while every family's contact details stay protected until the profile's own agent approves.

FOR REGISTERED AGENTS
New agents sign up with their mobile number (verified by OTP) and wait for an admin to activate the account. Every agent gets a permanent agent code for their State.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Browse all profiles shown to your communities, with filters for age, height, education, profession, income, star, raasi and more.
• Add profiles in four simple steps: personal, professional, horoscope, family & contact — with photos, cropping and the camera.
• Every profile gets a permanent ID, visible only to you, the admins and the family.
• Ask for contact details: the profile's own agent approves or declines, and approved details stay visible for 15 days.
• Share masked profiles on WhatsApp as a link or PDF — never with contact details or who uploaded them.
• Manage your clients from Wedbook Matrimony: approve new families, handle interests between families, and approve agent changes.
• Put a profile on hold, shortlist profiles, and get notified about every request.

PRIVACY BUILT IN
Families choose which details are visible before approval, and which communities may see their profile. Phone numbers, parents' details, native place and family details are shared only after approval — in the app and never in shares.

Admins activate agents, manage communities and masters, and oversee requests.
