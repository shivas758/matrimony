## Full description (max 4000)
Wedbook Matrimony connects families with trusted marriage bureau agents. Every profile is registered through an agent who knows the family, so you browse genuine profiles from the communities you care about — and personal contact details are shared only when both sides agree.

HOW IT WORKS
• Sign in with your mobile number and a one-time code (OTP). No password to remember.
• Look around as a guest: photo, age, job, community and package.
• Choose your marriage bureau agent and register one profile, in four simple steps: personal, professional, horoscope, family & contact.
• Your agent checks and approves your profile, and it goes live.
• Open full profiles, send interests to the families you like, and follow every reply.

PRIVACY FIRST
• Only the main photo (watermarked), age and gender are always shown.
• You decide which other details others can see — name, date of birth, education, income, horoscope, extra photos and more.
• Phone numbers, parents' and family details and native place are shared only after the other family's agent accepts your interest — and only for 15 days.
• Choose which communities your profile is shown to: your own, a few selected ones, or all.
• Who manages a profile is never shown to other families.

MADE FOR TELUGU AND KANNADA FAMILIES
Communities, sub-castes, gothram, star (nakshatram) and raasi, with a horoscope section built in.

YOUR OWN PROFILE ID
Each profile gets a permanent Wedbook ID, visible only to you and your agent.

Questions? Your agent's name and number are in the app, or visit https://matrimony-backend.duckdns.org/contact
